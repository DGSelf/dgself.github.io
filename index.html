The Problem & The Insight

Interoperable Avatars Break World Integrity

3D virtual worlds and cross-world avatars are exploding, but lack identity constraints. Without an enforcement layer for “what fits,” developers lose creative control and avoid avatar interoperability altogether.

Avatars Move Across Virtual Worlds

Technical standards allow users to bring external 3D avatars into new games and social spaces.

Zero Control Over Style, Role, or Immersion Fit

Cartoon or meme avatars enter realistic, lore-driven worlds (and vice versa), shattering atmosphere.

Developers Lose Creative Control → Avoid Interoperability

World creators lock down their ecosystems because open avatar imports threaten their artistic direction.
